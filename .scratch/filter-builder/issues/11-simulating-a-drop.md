# Simulating a drop: pool composition and the rarity slider

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 03

## Question

Simulation generates a random assortment of drops from toggleable pools - runes,
gems, sets, uniques, bases and whatever else - unidentified as they would drop,
with a slider biasing the sample toward the rare end.

Decide what the pools are and how a player toggles them. Decide what the slider
actually does: whether it weights toward the game's own rarity data, toward the
qlvl of a source, or toward a curated notion of what players care about, and what
its ends mean. Decide how many items a run produces, whether a run is repeatable
from a seed so a filter change can be judged against the same drops twice, and
how the result is drawn - as a list of labels, as an automap, or as both.

The point is to see what a session of play would look like under this filter,
which is a different question from testing one rule against one item.
