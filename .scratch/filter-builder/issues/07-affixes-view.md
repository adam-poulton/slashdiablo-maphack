# The Affixes view: what it shows, and what it is for in rule building

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 06

## Question

`AffixCatalogue` is read and registers `PrefixKind` and `SuffixKind` with the
stat index, and it is the one catalogue with no front end.

Decide what an Affixes view shows. A prefix and a suffix are unlike a unique or a
runeword: there are very many, they are grouped by the stat they grant and gated
by item level, group and rarity, and a player looking one up is usually asking
what can roll this and how high, rather than what this one does.

Decide how that shapes the view, and decide what it owes the builder
specifically: an affix is the thing a player is reasoning about when they write
a rule that gates on a stat range, so the connection to seeding a rule from what
is on screen has to be as deliberate as the one the unique and set views get.
